## Real work and public adaptations

- **Daily transitions:** based on a real working revision of a daily merchant-activity reporting procedure and its companion validation SQL. The revision addresses prior-day history, conflicting daily totals and reconciliation. Its own header says it was not executed on the employer's SQL Server; no later execution evidence was established for this case.
- **Source precedence:** based on a real working correction to overlapping source branches in an entity-universe procedure. The artifact contains source exclusion, ranked deduplication and controlled deployment logic. Its existence does not establish a successful deployment.

The original working artifacts remain private. The public files are reduced, redacted adaptations rather than verbatim production exports. Database and object names, internal role/channel labels, business thresholds, real identifiers, email recipients, server configuration and deployment details are omitted or replaced. Public fixture rows were created independently; they were not sampled, hashed or transformed from production records.

The adaptations also contain two explicitly documented review changes: missing-current records are exceptions in the transition example, and the precedence example uses a declared unique fixture key. These are public-review choices, not backdated claims about implementation at work.
