# Project ReL: making collaboration part of the work

**Status:** retrospective account, written 10 October 2026. Current project operation has not been established. This is a coordination case, not a software implementation claim.

Project ReL was an Enactus KNUST team initiative. The idea had been conceived by people before me during COVID. After school resumed, I helped revive it and move the collaboration forward.

My major role was stakeholder collaboration and coordination. That included the relationships between our university and the partner school, approvals from both schools, gathering resources, organizing videographers, and coordinating the student developers involved. The development work and the educators’ knowledge were essential contributions in their own right.

## What changed my approach

We came with an idea, but the partner school and teachers knew things that we could not learn from a technical plan alone. Their input mattered to what could be built and how the work could proceed. That meant accepting the limits of our initial understanding and making room for others to shape the response.

I had previously worked on a hospital design that was not implemented. The lesson I draw from these experiences is that having a plausible technical idea does not establish that the idea will work for the people expected to use it. Bringing the relevant people into the work changes the problem you think you are solving.

## What I would investigate next

I would want to understand which parts educators found useful, how maintenance responsibilities were assigned, and whether the resources remained usable after the initial team moved on. These are questions for a follow-up, not findings from an evaluation I have completed.
